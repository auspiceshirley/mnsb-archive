## Embedded Preview Images & Third-Party Cookies

For preview images embedded on this website (such as **link previews**, **video covers**, etc.), the loading process may transmit device information (such as your IP address) to third-party service providers or set/read Cookies. We categorize these resources under "**Third-Party Embedded Cookies**."

To protect your privacy, these images will only load after you have explicitly consented to the "**Third-Party Embedded Cookies**" authorization. Due to the wide variety and range of third-party sources, we cannot list each provider's privacy policy individually. Please visit the respective third-party websites to view their detailed terms.
