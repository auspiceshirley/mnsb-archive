## What kind of site is this?

This is a collection site for fan creations of "Magical Girl Witch Trials." <TextMask>Fun fact: this is a static site.</TextMask>

We call it an "archive," but for copyright reasons, we do not store or distribute any actual digital files of fan works—all our data comes from external sites. <TextMask>This is basically just the site author's "bookmarks."</TextMask>

If a work becomes unavailable on its original platform (taken down by copyright claim or deleted by the creator), then it's truly gone. <TextMask>Maybe you can find it on the Internet Archive?</TextMask>

## What are our criteria for inclusion?

—Pretty much none. It all depends on the site author's luck and personal taste. <TextMask>But judging by their daily intense internet browsing habits</TextMask>, the collection here will only keep growing.

One thing to note: we do not include NSFW content or unauthorized reposts. Our初衷 is to collect content that is suitable for general audiences and complies with copyright law. <TextMask>After all, I don't want to deal with DMCA takedowns.</TextMask>

Since this is just a personal site, updates are irregular. Could be daily, could be every few weeks—don't ask me how I know so much, <TextMask>because the person writing these words is the site author.</TextMask>

## Me and "Magical Girl Witch Trials"

Let me tell you a little story, <TextMask>and sneak in a bit of personal bias</TextMask>～🍎

The first time I came across this game was when that "blue-haired little girl" <TextMask>Tachibana Sherry</TextMask> meme was spreading like wildfire across social media. I had no idea who she was, so I searched—<TextMask>and that's how I fell into the rabbit hole of "Magical Girl Witch Trials"!</TextMask>

This was my very first game on Steam. It told such a beautiful story: <TextMask>no spoilers for you, hehe</TextMask>. I still remember that evening when I finished the game. I sat in front of my computer, feeling lost for a long, long time... It was like watching a long movie, or witnessing the end of an era. Scenes from the game flashed before my eyes, one after another. I clenched my teeth, refusing to cry—it was all like a dream.

Though the story in the game is over, its true "life" often lies not in the game itself, but in the衍生 works and collaborations it inspires. For players, these things often hold more value than the game itself. Even if you forget the plot, when you see fan creations, you can't help but smile.

That's why I went through all this trouble to build this site. When I see something good, I want to share it—it's truly a feast for the eyes and ears...

And no, <TextMask>this is not an ad</TextMask>. Good things always get promoted organically, hehe.
